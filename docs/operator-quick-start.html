Askel

Crew sea service and tickets, in one place.

A quick start for operators. Set up your company in a few minutes and see every crew member's progress toward their next AMSA near coastal ticket.

1

Create your company

On a computer or phone, open askel.com.au/dashboard.html, sign up with your work email, and choose Create company. You get a short company code for your crew.

2

Invite your crew

In the dashboard, tap Copy message and send it to your crew. It contains the code and instructions.

Crew open askel.com.au/app on their phone, create a free account in Settings, and enter your code under Your company. Crew pay nothing, ever.

3

Crew log time, then get it signed once

Crew log days as they work them, including casual and charter days, and the app counts them against AMSA's day requirements for each ticket.

Signing happens once they have finished the time they need, usually one signing per duty. The crew member sends a link to the master or engineer, who signs on their phone in about 30 seconds, with no account and no printing. Askel fills in the AMSA Form 771 with the supervisor's signature on it.

4

Read your dashboard

See who is ready to apply, who is close, and who is waiting on a signature. Sort and filter your crew, add private notes, and print a crew progress report.

Askel can also prepare a sea service letter and an application pack for each crew member who wants one.

What you see, and what you don't

You see

  • Names of crew who join your company
  • The tickets they hold and are working towards
  • Their days counted toward each ticket, and which are signed
  • Who is waiting on a signature

You do not see

  • Date of birth, phone or email
  • Their vessel history or entries
  • Anything from before they joined

Crew can leave your company at any time.

What if we stop?

Your dashboard locks. Your crew keep full use of the app and all of their records.

Does it replace the AMSA process?

No. Askel helps crew keep an accurate record and prepare their documents. They still apply through myAMSA, and AMSA makes every decision.

Where is the data kept?

On Australian servers (Sydney). Crew choose whether to share their progress with an employer.
