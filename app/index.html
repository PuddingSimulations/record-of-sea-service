Luxury yacht: extra evidence

AMSA may accept commercial service on a luxury yacht if you were paid and the duties relate to the certificate. Send these with your application, as well as the standard evidence:

  • A copy of the vessel’s Safety Management System with your signed induction, or a crew list or muster stations list with your name on it
  • Plus any of: a letter from the owner or operator (vessel details, paid employment, duties, period), your employment contract naming the vessel, duties and period, or pay slips
