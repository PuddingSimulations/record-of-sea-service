Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The short version: your sea service records belong to you. Askel stores them on your phone and, if you create an account, in the cloud so you can restore them. Your employer only sees what you choose to share with them. We do not sell your information and we do not run advertising.

Who we are

Askel is operated by [your full legal name or business name] (ABN [your ABN]), in Australia. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean the operator of Askel. You can contact us at hello@askel.com.au.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and a password. Passwords are stored in scrambled (hashed) form by our authentication provider. We cannot read them.
  • Your profile (optional): name, date of birth, phone, email, company, and a drawn signature image. You enter these to fill in AMSA forms.
  • Sea service records: vessels, dates, days, duties, vessel details, notes, and supervisor details and signatures that you enter or that a supervisor adds.
  • Settings: tickets you hold, tickets you are working towards, and your app preferences.
  • Supervisors (no account needed): when a supervisor signs through a link, we receive the name, position, certificate, phone, email, signature and date they enter on that page.
  • Employers: an email address, password and company name, plus the licence status of the company.
  • Technical data: our hosting and service providers may log your IP address and device type when your device connects. We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To run the app: calculate your progress, fill in AMSA Form 771 and sea service letters, and let supervisors sign.
  • To sync your records between your devices when you have an account.
  • To show your employer your progress, but only if you join a company with its code.
  • To send you account emails such as email confirmation and password reset.
  • To keep the service secure and to respond to support requests.

Who can see your information

  • You. Your records are yours.
  • Your supervisor: only the details of the entry or letter you send them (your name, the vessel, dates, days and duty).
  • Your employer: only if you join their company in the app. They see your name, the tickets you are working towards, how many days you have logged, and how many are signed. They do not see your date of birth, phone, email, or vessel history. You can leave a company at any time and they stop seeing your progress.
  • Our service providers, who handle information for us to run Askel: Supabase (database and sign-in), GitHub (website hosting), and a content delivery network (cdnjs) that supplies the PDF viewer when you preview a form, which can see your IP address. If we send emails, our email provider will also handle your email address.
  • AMSA: we do not send your records to AMSA. You submit your own application and documents to them.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Records are stored on your device. If you create an account, a copy is stored on servers operated by Supabase in [region, for example Sydney, Australia]. Some of our providers may process information overseas. We take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information and records while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings, Account, Delete my account). That removes your cloud records, settings, profile and sign-in. Copies may remain in routine backups for a short time. Records saved on your own device stay there until you remove them. If you created a company as an employer, deleting your account also deletes that company.

Security

Connections to Askel are encrypted. Access to cloud records is limited to your own account by database rules, and employers can only read the progress summary you share. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices and rights

You can access and correct your information in the app, export your records, and delete your account. You can also ask us for a copy of your information or to correct it by emailing us. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth). If you are not happy with how we handled a request or complaint, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Children

Askel is for working seafarers and the people who employ and supervise them. It is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, we will tell you in the app or by email.
